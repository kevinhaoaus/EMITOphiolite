Where these rocks can lock CO₂ into stone

Mantle rock in Oman can turn carbon dioxide into solid minerals. This map shows where serpentine and carbonate already are in one real satellite scene, so a storage team knows where to look.

Open the map and pick a mineral such as lizardite or calcite. Brighter ground means the satellite spectrum looks more like that mineral. A bright pixel is not a tonne of stored carbon, and it does not mean anyone owns the land.
